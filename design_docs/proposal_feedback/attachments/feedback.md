I think this project sounds cool, but I would spend a little bit more time on the motivation ... right now it comes across as you just want to answer one of three somewhat specific questions. I think in reality you are arguing that recommending the right data hygiene is challenging and involves many disparate workflows, but this didn't come across on first read.

I also wanted to call out that your evaluation sweep is quite ambitious so you may want to prune some models and harnesses. Still I think running many combinations and running (FAIRLY) with and without your tools is a good idea, and the general harnesses without the tools will be a good baseline.

Think about what are the 1-2 really AMAZING things you want from this project! Then you can think go what eval would really capture this.
