# Midway Demo — Video Link

## Q1 Demo video link

1 Point

**Grading comment:**

Complementary to the midway report, the midway demo is an opportunity to practice effectively communicating your findings.

### Guidelines:

For the midway demo presentation, please account for the following constraints:

1) Time limit of 3 minutes
2) We strongly encourage all team members to speak during the presentation.
3) You may choose whatever presentation medium feels most effective at communicating your work (e.g., slides, website, live-demo of experiments/methods running in the terminal, training curves, evaluation logs).
4) We recommend using visualizations and demos of the project code and relevant artifacts when possible.

### Grading:

We will grade you with respect to the quality of the presentation. Your presentation should be clear and easy to follow. While the midway report is graded on the progress made and the report quality, with the midway demo you are being graded on how you convey these outcomes to a small audience.

### How to submit:

1. Upload your recorded demo to YouTube as an unlisted video, or to Google Drive with viewing access for course staff.
2. Copy the video sharing URL. For Google Drive, open Share, ensure the course staff can view the file, then click Copy link. For YouTube, copy the unlisted video URL.
3. Paste the full URL (including https://) into the response field below. Submit the link, rather than uploading the video file to Gradescope.
4. Check that the video plays with sound and that staff can open the link without requesting access. Keep the video available through grading.
5. Submit once per team and add all team members to the Gradescope submission (maximum 3 members).
