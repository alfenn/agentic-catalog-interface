# CS 329Z: Engineering AI Agents

## Midway Report

With the midway report, we’d like to see a report that reflects meaningful progress on your project along with a projection of what remaining ogoals you’d like to hit by the final submission of the project.

## Guidelines

Your project proposal should be 3-4 pages long using the following template (or ICLR template as you used in the proposal phase). We recommend your report abide by the following structure:

- **Title, Author(s)**
- **Problem Statement:** Clearly define what research question(s) you are addressing with this project. This should summarize and sharpen the thoughts you put down in the Proposal. We’re looking for a precise and concise recap.
- **Methodology:** On top of what was stated in the Proposal, describe the concrete approaches you’ve taken so far. We’d like to see tangible evidence of your progress. Describing approaches that didn’t work out are very welcome as well!
- **Results:** Put preliminary, intermediate results in this section. Include details like your experimental setup, the models / datasets / benchmarks you evaluated on, and metrics, wherever applicable.
- **Assets:** Provide any links (e.g., GitHub, websites, HuggingFace) to artifacts that you’ve created that reflect the progress discussed in your report. You may also link a zip of your repository if you don’t wish to release it publicly on these platforms yet.
- **Timeline:** For milestones you plan to hit throughout the rest of the quarter.

## Grading

We’ll grade on a scale of 1 to 5, where the score reflects the quality of the report in terms of its thoroughness and readability, along with the level of progress shown so far. Progress is defined purely as the level of effort put into the work, as reflected by the artifacts you’ve surfaced. While positive outcomes and results are exciting, we do not define progress as such.
